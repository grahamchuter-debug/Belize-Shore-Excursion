Pricing and guests

  • Adults (12+) — USD $115
  • Children (6–11) — USD $85
  • Infants are not permitted. At least one adult is required
  • Children under 12 wear a life jacket and must be accompanied by an adult
  • Online requests accept up to 10 guests. For larger groups email hello@belizeshoreexcursion.com

Book now

What is included

  • Two snorkel sites with about 90 minutes of water time
  • Caye Caulker island time

Not included: lunch. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

Access and fitness

  • Not wheelchair accessible
  • Guests must step into the vehicle, climb onto the boat and be able to swim

Meeting point is about a two-minute walk from the tender pier. Exact instructions are provided after confirmation.

How booking works

  1. Choose your cruise date and guests
  2. Pay securely online to submit your request
  3. We check the excursion and email confirmation separately
  4. If we cannot confirm, you receive a full refund

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 14 days before your excursion. Cancellations made within 14 days of departure are non-refundable. If we are unable to confirm your excursion after payment, you will receive a full refund to your original payment method.
