Belize Private Tours for Cruise Passengers

Private and small-group formats can help families and friends keep a shared pace on a tender-port day.

Compare excursions

Private and small-group hub

Use flexibility to simplify the day

Private or small-group formats can improve pacing and communication, but they do not shorten Belize's roads, boat transfers or tender process.

The inventory contains one explicitly private route—Private Tubing Expedition—and several small-group routes. Treat those labels distinctly and confirm the actual operating format for your date.

  • Share mobility and activity needs.
  • Agree the route focus before leaving port.
  • Keep a conservative return margin.

Private and small-group route guides

Altun-Ha and Jeep Adventure

Climb temples at Altun-Ha, explore a cave system and enjoy a thrilling Jeep ride through the Belizean countryside. 6 Hours · Moderate.

Jungle Jeep Adventure

An UN-BELIZEABLE Jeep experience to hidden parts of nature's best kept secrets in Belize. 4 Hours · Moderate.

Baboon Sanctuary and Jeep Adventure

Watch Black Howler Monkeys at the Community Baboon Sanctuary then a Jeep ride through the countryside. 5 Hours 30 Minutes · Moderate.

Belize Zoo and Jeep Adventure

See Chiqui the Jaguar cub, Fuego the Tapir and more at the Belize Zoo, then a Jeep countryside ride. 5 Hours · Moderate.

City Sightseeing and Highlights

Colonial capital views, beach time, Memorial Park and Baron Bliss Lighthouse on a cultural city tour. 2 Hours · Easy.

Cave Tubing and Jungle Trek with Lunch

Float through limestone caves that once served as Mayan ceremonial grounds with a jungle trek and lunch. 5 Hours · Moderate.

Xunantunich and Cave Tubing

Explore Xunantunich ruins and journey down a river through underground caves on this cultural tour. 6 Hours 30 Minutes · Moderate.

Private Tubing Expedition

Private tubing through interconnected cave systems with stalactites, stalagmites and captivating formations. 5 Hours · Difficult.
