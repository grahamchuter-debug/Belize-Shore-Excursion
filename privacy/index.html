Privacy

How we handle personal data

This site provides cruise planning information and, for selected excursions, a request-to-book journey.

If you email hello@belizeshoreexcursion.com, your address and message are used to respond to the enquiry.

If you submit a booking request, we process the contact and passenger details you provide, together with payment information handled by Stripe, so we can receive your request, arrange the excursion and communicate about confirmation or refunds. We do not sell personal data.

Payment card data is handled by Stripe — we do not store full card numbers on this site.

Hosting and security services may process standard technical logs such as IP address, user agent and requested URL.

Questions about privacy may be sent to hello@belizeshoreexcursion.com.
