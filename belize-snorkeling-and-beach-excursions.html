Belize Snorkeling and Beach Excursions

Match reef time, island pace and boat logistics to your tender schedule from Fort Street Tourism Village.

Plan one day in Belize

Reef and beach hub

Decide how much of the day belongs in the water

Belize routes range from snorkel-led reef days to beach-led island or city combinations.

A reef boat commonly takes about 45–60 minutes. Weather, sea state and visibility can change the plan, and wildlife encounters are possibilities rather than guarantees.

  • Compare swimming intensity and time ashore.
  • Plan for sun and a wet boat environment.
  • Confirm equipment and marine-site rules directly.
