Belize Mayan Ruins Excursions

Temple days inland from the tender pier — from compact Altun Ha visits to river-access Lamanai.

Explore guides

Mayan ruins hub

Compare sites by distance and depth

Altun Ha is the compact cruise-day choice; Lamanai, Cahal Pech and Xunantunich ask more of the port window.

Altun Ha is typically 45–60 minutes from Belize City. Lamanai is roughly 1.5 hours away and adds a river approach. The listed Cahal Pech and Xunantunich routes run six hours or more, so check the tender schedule before treating them as interchangeable.

Four archaeological directions

Altun Ha

The closest featured ruins route, with city context in a listed four-hour day.

Lamanai

A longer river-and-ruins route for a generous port call.

Cahal Pech

A listed six-hour cultural route with Belize City context.

Xunantunich

A listed six-and-a-half-hour combination with cave tubing.

Altun Ha variations

These overlapping routes preserve different second themes—city, river, wildlife, zoo or Jeep. Compare the companion stop and listed duration rather than assuming duplication means the same experience.
