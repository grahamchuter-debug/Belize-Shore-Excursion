Pricing and guests

  • Eligible guests — USD $86 each
  • Infants — not permitted
  • All participants must be at least 8 years old and at least 48 inches tall
  • Online requests accept up to 10 guests. For larger groups email hello@belizeshoreexcursion.com

Book now

What is included

  • Guide and air-conditioned transport
  • Tube and miner's lamp
  • About two hours of cave float time

Not included: food and drinks.

Physical requirements

  • About a 30-minute walk on loose gravel
  • River wading on a rocky bottom; fitness required
  • Guests with neck, back or hip ailments, and anyone who is pregnant, should not participate

Meeting point: Cruise Ship Tender Pier. Exact instructions are provided after confirmation.

How booking works

  1. Choose your cruise date and guests
  2. Pay securely online to submit your request
  3. We check the excursion and email confirmation separately
  4. If we cannot confirm, you receive a full refund

Cancellation

Free cancellation up to 14 days before your excursion. Cancellations made within 14 days of departure are non-refundable. If we are unable to confirm your excursion after payment, you will receive a full refund to your original payment method.
